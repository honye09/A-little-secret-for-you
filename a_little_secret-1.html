A little secret...

You know the quote, “Nothing is a coincidence. Every soul you meet is written in your destiny—to teach you, to heal you, or to love you.”

But I wanted to write my own destiny with you, forever.

Sometimes, I sit in places where I can see you, just to make my day a little better. The smile on your face that makes your eyes smaller, your overwhelming cuteness, the mole on your right cheek, and the sound of your voice—I notice all of it.

It’s never been just attraction. What I feel for you feels like pure love. Every time I see you, my heart races. I suddenly become quiet and act like I don’t even know you, while inside, I completely forget how to behave normally. My pupils widen, my breath seems to stop for a second, and suddenly I don’t know what to do with myself.

I’ve noticed every little thing about you—the way you walk, the way you talk, the way you behave, and even the way you walk past me.

Every Tuesday during lunchtime, I wait at the same spot just to see you, while secretly hiding from you at the same time. 🙈

You’re like paracetamol to me because the moment I see you, I feel relieved, as if all my worries and pain disappear for a while.

Have you ever noticed me, even just once?

How do you feel about me?

Have you ever thought about me?
